# Using C4 as the gold benchmark

C4 is a quality reference, not a content template. Always read the current files rather than relying on this summary:

- `src/data/lessons/counting-probability/C4.json`
- `src/data/lessons/generated-problems/C4.json`
- the shared lesson renderer and resolution components referenced by `SKILL.md`.

## What to extract

Evaluate a new lesson against C4's abstract qualities:

- **Discovery-first pacing:** a concrete attempt creates a real uncertainty before the method is named.
- **Epistemic repair:** the lesson distinguishes seeing examples, organizing them, and proving the claim.
- **Student language:** questions are natural before technical terms are introduced.
- **Interactive thought:** sorting and choices perform mathematical work rather than decorate prose.
- **Selective motion:** animation shows paths or representation changes; still structures remain inspectable.
- **Worked resolutions:** substantial answers receive a visual or useful animation, ordered reasoning, and a transferable takeaway.
- **Representation continuity:** adjacent beats preserve the objects and labels under discussion.
- **Authentic integration:** a purpose-built example prepares the strategy, then the mapped problem becomes the natural next question.
- **Independent practice:** original problems vary the setting and decision, while the bank transfer remains authentic.
- **Ending:** the lesson consolidates its method, then changes one familiar condition to create the next unresolved tension.

## What not to copy

Do not mechanically copy:

- the movement-sequence puzzle;
- digit/zero contexts;
- casework language when the target idea is different;
- C4's beat count or exact phases;
- its visual primitives when another medium is clearer;
- its practice surfaces or answer patterns;
- its successor hook.

Instead ask of the requested node:

1. What is this lesson's equivalent of C4's genuine uncertainty?
2. What could the learner manipulate to resolve it?
3. What proof obligation or misconception needs explicit repair?
4. What representation should survive into the authentic problem?
5. What compact recap captures this method's recognition cues and failure modes?
6. What familiar object can be changed slightly to invite the successor?

Matching C4 means matching the causal and explanatory quality, not its story assets.
