# Routine Lesson Quality Card

Read this only when the compact context packet and the quality rules in `SKILL.md` leave a routine design choice unclear.

## Plan the investigation

Answer five questions before drafting prose:

1. What useful representation or habit can the learner carry in from the predecessor?
2. What tempting misconception should the opening make visible?
3. What action or comparison will create the new realization?
4. Where will the learner explain or apply it independently?
5. What unresolved variation naturally belongs to the named successor?

Use a generated problem when low context load makes the mechanism easier to discover. Use an approved bank problem when its authentic structure adds a valuable guided application or transfer test. Never use authenticity as a substitute for fit.

## Choose the lightest effective medium

- **Interaction:** the learner's choice, grouping, movement, or construction produces evidence.
- **Animation:** the mathematical meaning depends on change over time or a transformation between representations.
- **Static visual:** a stable diagram, grid, table, or grouping exposes structure best through inspection.
- **Text:** the idea is already visible and another medium would add decoration rather than evidence.

Keep a representation alive across beats when the learner is changing how they interpret it. A new visual should answer the current prompt immediately.

## Make reasoning complete

When counting by cases, establish both coverage and non-overlap. When dividing an overcount, establish uniform multiplicity. When using a complement, establish the whole and the exact opposite. Apply the corresponding proof obligation for other techniques; examples alone are not a completeness argument.

A substantial response should lead to:

- feedback that addresses the attempted reasoning;
- a short worked resolution showing why the answer follows;
- one memorable takeaway that states the reusable idea.

## Read the finished lesson aloud

The sequence should sound like one tutor continuing a shared investigation. Remove author-facing rationale from learner copy, eliminate generated/bank duplicates, verify prompt-to-visual synchronization, consolidate the main idea, and only then open the successor's question.
